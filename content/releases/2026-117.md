### Jump Survey Protocol

Star system records now carry a Jump Survey panel. Operators may initiate a
localised cartographic scan at radii J-1 through J-6, producing a hex map
centred on the designated system and clipped to the precise hexagonal boundary
of the selected jump envelope. The resulting chart is rendered as a PNG
composite and presented in an overlay panel. A signed SVG embed link is provided
for integration into external displays or briefing materials.

The survey panel appears on both star system records and on stellar object
records that belong to a charted system. Jump maps are cached against the
current state of all parsecs, systems, and regions within the viewport;
subsequent requests within the same configuration state incur no regeneration
cost.
