### Public Interface Specification

The Survey Office has released a complete Interface Specification for the Survey Database, available at the `/api` directive of any installation. The specification catalogues every query and instruction the database will accept: sectors, subsectors, parsecs, star systems, stellar objects, regions, routes, jump logs, ships, commerce, survey overlays and search.

A machine-readable copy of the specification is supplied for use with external equipment and programming aids.
