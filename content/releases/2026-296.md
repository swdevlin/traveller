### Sector Subsector Matrix Display

Advances in long-range sector survey telemetry now allow the tabular subsector
register on the sector terminal to be superseded by a four-by-four matrix of
subsector cards. Each card presents summary data about its subsector at a
glance.

### Atmospheric Composition and Hydrographic Liquid Readouts

Refinements to onboard survey instrumentation, together with improved access to
the planetary infonet, now enable the planetary profile to report atmospheric
composition and the hydrographic liquid, where these are recorded. The readouts
are presented in the interactive navigation display sidebar and the analysis
detail panel. The same fields are included in the exported Markdown profile and
in the help terminal documentation.
