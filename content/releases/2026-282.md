### Orbital Plotting

The orbital plotter has been fitted to each star's survey record, and projects the bodies that circle the star onto concentric rings, nearest first, in the colours of the operator's chosen display. Each body is selectable, and its record opens on demand. The plot may be downloaded as an image, or requested by external instruments under the campaign's clearance token, which returns a plot free of selectable links.

### Single-Star Charts

The schematic chart of a star system may now be drawn for a single star, showing only the bodies in its own keeping. A star circling another appears as one mark, its own bodies being charted on its own record. The chart carries a shareable public link, as the system chart does.
