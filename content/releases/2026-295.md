### Stellar Body Registration

New terrestrial planets, planetoids, planetoid belts, gas giants and moons may
now be registered manually within an existing star system, without recourse to
the generator service. Moons are registered against a designated parent body.
Reference documentation for the amendment procedure is available from the help
terminals for each body classification.

### Ring Classification Support

Ring systems are now recognised as discrete records within the survey database,
and are displayed in orbital tables and cartographic output alongside the bodies
they encircle. Rings may be registered and amended in the same manner as other
stellar bodies.

### Orbit Sequence Correction

A fault has been rectified whereby the orbital ordering of a star system was
disturbed when a stellar body was regenerated. The sequence is now recomputed
upon regeneration, and a maintenance procedure is provided for the restoration
of ordering in existing records.

### Malformed Archive Handling

Tab-delimited archive files containing an invalid hex coordinate are now
rejected with a diagnostic message, rather than halting the import procedure.
