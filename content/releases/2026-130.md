### Sector Cartographic Poster

The system now produces a full-sector cartographic poster in portable document
format. The poster renders the complete 32×40 parsec grid at A4 dimensions,
incorporating all star systems, rogue bodies, communication network links,
regional overlays, and jump route indicators. Adjacent subsector designations
are printed along each border to facilitate cross-reference with neighbouring
sectors. The document is retrieved via the sector record and delivered as a
downloadable PDF artefact.
