### Sophont Registry Protocol

A central registry of sophont species is now maintained within the Data Cores.
Each registered species carries a designation, a language classification, a home
system, referee and player annotations, and a pictorial likeness. Worlds declared
to harbour a native sophont population may be assigned a species from the registry;
where none is on record, a designation entered at the point of assignment is
registered automatically, with the originating system declared as its home.

The assigned designation is displayed wherever the native sophont classification
appears. Within the interactive navigation display, selecting the designation
reveals the associated annotations. Referee annotations are released to the
referee alone; players are furnished with player annotations only.
