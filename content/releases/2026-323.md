### Bilateral Trade Number Protocol

The commerce terminal can now assess the strength of trade between neighbouring star systems. The new **Bilateral Trade Number (BTN)** provides a measure of commercial opportunity, taking into account local economic conditions, cultural attitudes and interstellar distance. Each assessment is accompanied by one of five classifications: Opportunistic, Low, Moderate, High or Very High.

The BTN protocol is an optional house rule and is disengaged by default. Referees may activate it under **House Rules** in Campaign Settings. Full operating instructions are available in the [Operations Manual](/help/bilateral_trade_number) and directly from the Trade Analysis display.
