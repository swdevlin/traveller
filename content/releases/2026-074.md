### System Chart Installed in Navigation Console

The star system display in the navigation sidebar has been replaced with a
vertical transit-line diagram — a schematic representation of each system's
orbital structure. Bodies are laid out top-to-bottom along a central spine in
order of orbital radius, with secondary stars branching into indented sub-spines
at their respective positions.

Body type is indicated by node geometry and colour: stars by large coloured
circles calibrated to spectral class, gas giants by banded discs, terrestrial
worlds by colour-coded circles reflecting atmospheric and hydrographic
character, and planetoid belts by the standard debris-field glyph. AU distances
are annotated along each connecting segment, and the jump shadow boundary is
marked where applicable.

Clicking a body node opens the full analysis detail panel for that object.
Selecting the ⊙ icon designates the body as the origin for in-system transit
calculations, displaying estimated transit times at 4G acceleration against all
other bodies in the chart. Stellar object visibility remains governed by the
system survey index.
