### Campaign Languages

The linguistic archive now accepts languages compiled by the campaign operator. A list of
words, entered directly or supplied as a text file, is analysed by the name synthesiser, which
then produces new names in the manner of that list. Campaign languages appear alongside the
standard languages, sorted by name, and may be reviewed, amended, retrieved or struck from the
record.

### Allegiance Languages

Each allegiance may now be assigned a language. Systems without a language of their own adopt
that of their allegiance before falling back to the subsector, sector or campaign default,
and newly surveyed systems are named accordingly.
