### Orbital Advancement Directive

The Survey Office has introduced a directive for the advancement of a star system through time. The new **Advance Orbits** command, available from the actions menu of any star system, accepts a number of days and carries every body within the system along its orbit by that interval.

Each body travels according to its own orbital period. Those of retrograde classification, moons of negative period among them, proceed in the opposite direction. A negative number of days returns all bodies to an earlier position. Bodies lacking a recorded period or position remain undisturbed.

The cumulative interval of advancement is recorded and displayed upon the system page, and the orbital charts are redrawn to reflect the new positions.
