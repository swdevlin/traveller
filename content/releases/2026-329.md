### Orbital Ephemeris Adjustment

The Imperial Survey Office has authorised a new procedure for the calculation of stellar system positions at dates other than the original survey data.

The **Advance Orbits** command, available from the actions menu of any star system, permits the operator to specify an interval in standard days. All recorded planetary bodies are repositioned according to their individual orbital periods, preserving their relative motions through the system.

The total elapsed interval from the original survey data is maintained in the system record.

### Automatic Ephemeris Advancement on Revisit

A new campaign directive, **Advance Orbits on Visit**, is now provided under the campaign configuration. The directive is held in the disengaged state by default.

Where the directive is engaged, the recording of a jump into a system previously visited by the same vessel causes the ephemeris of that system to be advanced automatically. The interval applied is the number of standard days elapsed between the vessel's previous arrival and the present one. A first arrival at a system leaves its recorded positions unaltered, as does the recording of a jump into any other system.

Should the jump record be subsequently deleted, the advancement is reversed and the bodies of the system are restored to their former positions. The orbit charts and the interactive navigation display now refresh to reflect each advancement.
