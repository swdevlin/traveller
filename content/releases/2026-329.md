### Orbital Ephemeris Adjustment

The Imperial Survey Office has authorised a new procedure for the calculation of stellar system positions at dates other than the original survey data.

The **Advance Orbits** command, available from the actions menu of any star system, permits the operator to specify an interval in standard days. All recorded planetary bodies are repositioned according to their individual orbital periods, preserving their relative motions through the system.

The total elapsed interval from the original survey data is maintained in the system record.
