### Automatic Subsector Scan Protocol

Newly registered sectors now undergo automatic subsector scans. Upon creation,
each of the sixteen subsectors is committed to the scan queue without operator
intervention, and the sector is charted progressively as the Data Cores complete
each assignment. Sectors imported from the external cartographic archive are
always scanned in this manner.

The scan directive is engaged by default. It may be disengaged at the point of
creation, in which case the sector is registered without scanning and the
operator may initiate scans at a later time.

### Sector Registration Parameters

The sector commissioning console now offers a selection of registration methods. A
single set of survey parameters may be applied across the whole sector, or each
subsector may be assigned parameters of its own. Sectors may also be registered
as uncharted, containing no scanned systems until the operator directs otherwise.
