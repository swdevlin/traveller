### Starport Capacity Register

The Survey Office has expanded its starport records to include detailed information on the facilities and capabilities of ports serving populated worlds.

The new **Starport Capacity Register** identifies the presence of a highport, the docking capacities of highport and downport facilities, and the tonnage accommodated within internal docking bays. Where shipyards are present, the register records the classes of vessels they are equipped to construct, their maximum build capacity, and their annual production output.
