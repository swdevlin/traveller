### Survey Archive Capital Records

Capital designations are now available through the Imperial survey archive.
 Cartographic displays can use these records to identify sector and 
subsector capitals and apply appropriate chart notation automatically.

### Variable Chart Scale

The astrogation display now supports alternate hex scales, permitting
 operators to contract or enlarge the standard lattice according to 
 operational requirements.
