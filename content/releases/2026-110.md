### Extended Government Classification

Survey records for inhabited worlds now carry a richer government profile.
Where deep analysis data is available, the Government tab presents three
additional classifications beyond the standard type and description.

### Economic Profile

Where deep survey data is available, worlds now display a dedicated Economics
section within the Starport tab.

### Cultural Characteristics

Where cultural analysis data is available, the Population tab now presents a
dedicated Culture section.
