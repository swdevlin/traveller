### Sophont Check Protocol

A *Sophont Check* parameter is now configurable per campaign via the Campaign
Settings panel. The setting governs the sophont check procedure applied during
star system generation, in accordance with the World Builder's Handbook (p.130).
Three modes are available: **Standard** — the default generation procedure;
**Rare Earth Universe Variant** — substantially reduces the probability of
complex sophont life arising; and **None** — sophont checks are suppressed
entirely. Charted Space campaigns default to *None*; all other
campaign types default to *Standard*.

### Survey Index Tracking

Jump logs now support setting the *Survey Index* value at the
destination parsec.
