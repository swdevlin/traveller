### Trade Synergy Analysis

The commerce terminal now offers a Trade Synergy analysis for any registered star system. Given a jump range, the analysis surveys every neighbouring system within reach and determines which goods may be carried profitably between the origin and each neighbour, in both directions. Results are tabulated by neighbour, with export and import counts and their cumulative bonus, and may be ordered by distance, name, or any of the totals. Selecting a row reveals the individual goods and the bonus attached to each. A cartographic view presents the same findings upon the navigation display.

The analysis concerns ordinary trade only. A good is considered only where it is reliably procurable at the world of origin: the Common Goods, together with those Trade Goods whose availability classification matches the world's trade codes. Speculative lots procured by chance, according to population, are excluded.
