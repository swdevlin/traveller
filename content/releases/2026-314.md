### Cartographic Batch Directive

The Cartographic Office has introduced a new facility for preparing planetary maps across an entire star system.

Rather than commissioning charts for individual worlds, referees may now select **Generate Maps** from the star system's Actions menu to produce maps for all eligible planets, planetoids and moons in a single operation.

Existing charts, including those supplied by the referee, are preserved. New maps become available as they are completed, allowing the referee to continue surveying the system while the cartographers attend to their work.
