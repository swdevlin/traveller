### Cartographic Batch Directive

A new Generate Maps directive is now available beneath the Actions menu of every
star system record. Upon selection, a surface chart is requisitioned for each
terrestrial planet, planetoid and moon of size 1 or greater that does not yet
hold one. Charts already on file, whether generated or supplied by the referee,
are left undisturbed. The charts are prepared in sequence and appear upon the
page of each world as they are completed. The directive is withheld when no
chart remains to be prepared.

The remaining star system controls have been consolidated beneath the same
Actions menu, adjacent to the Edit directive at the head of the record.
