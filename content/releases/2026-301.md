### Object Tag Designation Protocol

Survey objects may now carry tag designations: free-form labels attached to
sectors, subsectors, regions, star systems, stars, and every class of stellar
body. Tags are assigned from the sidebar of an object's survey record, where
multiple designations may be entered at once, separated by commas. Designations
already held in the register are offered as suggestions during entry, and an
assigned tag is voided from the record by a single directive.

Tag designations are restricted to the campaign referee. They are withheld from
all other operators across survey records, listings, and the data interface.

### Tag Registry

A consolidated register of all tag designations is now maintained for each
campaign. Each entry lists the designation, its descriptive text, and the count
of objects bearing it. Selecting an entry displays every object so classified,
grouped by type. A designation may be renamed, annotated with a description, or
purged; purging a designation removes it from all objects in the campaign.

### Tag Selection Parameter

Cartographic filters and highlight rules accept a new parameter, Tags,
permitting systems to be selected by designation. A system satisfies the
parameter when it, or any star, planet, moon, or other body within it, carries
the designation sought. Designations held by the enclosing sector, subsector,
or region do not qualify.
