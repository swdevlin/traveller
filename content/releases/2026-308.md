### Row Directive Menu

The directives available against each star system and rogue object in the subsector tables have been consolidated into a single menu, opened by the control at the end of the row. Edit, Add Tag, and Delete are retained and are joined by the Move directive.

### Relocation Directive

A star system or rogue object may now be relocated to another hex. The operator selects the destination by selecting a hex on a chart of the surrounding parsecs, in the manner of the jump route link chart, or supplies the sector and four-digit hex designation of a more distant destination, which may lie across a sector border. A star system will not be relocated into a hex already occupied by another star system. Jump route records are not altered by relocation.
