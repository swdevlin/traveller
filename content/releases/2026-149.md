### Campaign Settings — Imperial Date Format Selection

Campaign operators may now designate the preferred format for the display of Imperial dates throughout the interface. Two formats are supported:

**Traveller Standard** presents dates in day-year notation (e.g. `001-1105`), consistent with the conventional Traveller date convention. **ISO 8601** presents dates in year-day notation (e.g. `1105-001`). The setting is configured under the *Display Preferences* section of Campaign Settings and defaults to Traveller Standard on all new and existing campaigns.
The selected format is applied uniformly across all date-bearing displays: the Jump Log Registry table, individual jump log detail records, and the jump log show and edit console headers.

### Sector Registry — Chart Projection Mode

The IISS Sector Registry now provides a secondary display mode: the Chart Projection. Operators may toggle between the existing tabular list and the new spatial grid using the mode selector controls positioned at the upper-right of the registry console.

In Chart Projection mode, each registered sector occupies a cell in a coordinate-accurate grid, rendered at its recorded galactic position. Sector designation and coordinates are displayed within each cell. Activating a cell navigates the operator directly to that sector's detail record.
