### Campaign Classification System Installed

Campaigns are now assigned a type at the point of creation: *Charted Space*,
*Deepnight Revelation*, or *Homebrew*. The creation form presents contextual
guidance for each classification and, where applicable, prompts selection of a
sector data source — either TravellerMap or the Deepnight Revelation sourcebook
defaults. Campaign type and sector source may be amended at any time via the
Campaign Settings panel.

### Deepnight Revelation Auto-Initialisation

When a Deepnight Revelation campaign is created, the system automatically
dispatches sector creation and build script assignment across the full Deepnight
operational theatre. No manual intervention is required; sectors and subsectors
are constructed and configured in the background upon campaign initialisation.

### Campaign Settings Panel

A Campaign Settings panel has been added to the Settings area, presenting
current campaign classification, sector data source, and subsector counts. The
panel provides the following operational controls:

- **Set Build Scripts** — assigns build configurations to all subsectors based
  on the campaign's sector source.
- **Populate Empty Sectors** — dispatches generation orders only for sectors
  that contain no star systems. The confirmation prompt states the count of
  affected sectors and subsectors prior to execution.
- **Populate All Sectors** — dispatches generation orders for all subsectors
  that carry a build plan, regardless of current population status.
