### Planetary Size Classification Selector

The terrestrial planet size code field has been upgraded from a manual numeric entry
to a classification selector displaying size codes 1–F with corresponding average
diameters per standard IISS planetary survey tables. Selecting a size code
automatically populates the diameter field with the catalogue average for that
classification. The diameter remains operator-adjustable for fine calibration.

### Planetary Mass and Gravity Auto-Computation

Modifications to a terrestrial planet's diameter or density now trigger automatic
recalculation of surface gravity and planetary mass. Gravity is derived from the
density–diameter ratio relative to standard Terra, and mass from the cubic ratio.
These computed values are pre-filled in their respective fields but remain editable
should the operator require manual override.
