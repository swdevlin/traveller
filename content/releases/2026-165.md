### Transit Matrix Directive — Star System Survey Console

The star system survey console now presents a Transit Matrix readout, cross-referencing all charted bodies in the system
by orbital sequence classification. The operator selects a manoeuvre drive rating via the parameter control; all transit
time values in the matrix update accordingly.

Planetoids and moons are excluded from the matrix in accordance with standard navigational doctrine.

### Transit Times Readout — Individual Body Classification

Transit time data is now accessible from the individual body classification display for stellar objects. 
The orbital parameters panel surfaces a Transit Times readout listing calculated travel durations from the current body 
to all other surveyed objects in the system.
