### Navigation Console Activated

The interactive navigation console is now operational and accessible to all
campaign personnel — both players and referees — without requiring system
authentication. The console may be distributed to crew as a live tactical
display.

Data presented to players is governed by the survey index recorded for each
star system. Systems with incomplete or absent survey data will display
accordingly; no information is disclosed beyond what the survey record supports.

Referee sessions receive full access to all charted data. In addition, the
console highlights systems carrying confirmed native sophont populations or
evidence of extinct sophont civilisations, permitting rapid identification of
xenologically significant targets during mission planning.
