### Sector Registration: Settlement Classification Directive

When filing a new blank sector with the IISS Registry, operators may now
apply a settlement classification to the sector prior to commitment.

Sectors designated as **settled space** are provisioned with a standard
populated build specification and a survey index of 10, indicating full
data availability on crew-accessible charts. Sectors designated as
**exploration space** receive a minimal build specification and a survey
index of 3, consistent with regions not yet formally entered in the Grand
Survey.

The applicable classification is inferred from the active campaign type
and may be overridden by the registering operator before the sector record
is committed to the registry.
