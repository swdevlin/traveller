### Elliptical Orbit Rendering

The tactical display's orbital diagram no longer renders every path as a
perfect circle. Where a body's course is eccentric, the diagram recovers the
true ellipse — its shape, tilt, and offset from centre — so orbit rings on
the display now match the charted path.

### Orbital Mechanics Refinement

Periapsis and apoapsis distances now accompany every orbital record,
computed from the body's true path rather than assumed from a circular
approximation. Jump shadow reckonings for planets, moons, and their
satellites likewise draw upon each body's actual charted position,
correcting prior estimates for orbits of high eccentricity.

### Reference Link Register

Sector, subsector, and star system records now accept an operator-entered
reference link, held independently of the standard wiki citation. Where no
entry is made, and the campaign draws upon Charted Space archives, the
citation is supplied automatically. The link appears in the record's summary
panel and, for star systems, upon the tactical display's own detail sidebar.
