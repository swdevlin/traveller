### Allegiance Assignment via Social Characteristics Protocol

The Assign Social Characteristics directive now accepts an allegiance parameter.
Operators may search and select a polity classification at the time of world
socialisation, causing the designated main world to be registered under the
specified allegiance upon confirmation. Selection is optional; omitting the
parameter leaves any existing allegiance classification unchanged.

### Social Characteristics Preset Registry

The Assign Social Characteristics directive now supports named parameter presets.
Operators conducting batch socialisation operations across multiple worlds may
record a full parameter set — including population thresholds, government and
law level constraints, tech level bounds, main world criteria, captive government
permission, and allegiance — under a designated designation string. Saved presets
are recalled from the Preset selector at the top of the directive panel and
applied in a single operation. Presets are stored per campaign and persist across
sessions. Individual presets may be removed via the delete control adjacent to the
selector.
