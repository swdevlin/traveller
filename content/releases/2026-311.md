### Initial Sector Selection Directive

A newly commissioned Charted Space campaign need no longer commence from an empty chart. The referee designates a starting sector by name, and an uplink to the Traveller Map data banks retrieves its systems, routes, and allegiances and commits them to the chart library before the first survey is ever run — the installation standing ready for exploration, not assembly.

### Account Termination Directive

A referee may now terminate their own account from the Danger Zone classification of Campaign Settings. Termination requires password confirmation and, once authorised, permanently removes the account and its associated campaign.

### Deepnight Revelation Sector Operations

Deepnight Revelation campaigns gain three new sector-maintenance directives on the Sectors index: creation of any predefined sector not yet charted, regeneration of all sectors with an option to preserve visited parsecs, and population of charted but empty sectors.
