### Sophont Survey Indicators

The star system registry table and the orbital body survey table on the star system
  display now include sophont presence indicators alongside survey statistics. Systems
  and bodies with confirmed native sophont populations are marked with a native sophont
  glyph. Those with evidence of extinct sophont civilisations carry a corresponding
  extinction marker. Both indicators are sourced from biological survey data and
  require no additional operator input.
