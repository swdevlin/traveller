### Message Transit Time Protocol

The Travel Times terminal of the navigation display now includes a Transmit
directive, positioned alongside the M-Drive rating selectors. When engaged, the
transit grid ceases to report vessel travel durations and instead reports the
time required for a light-speed message to traverse the distance between each
pair of bodies within the system.

Selecting any M-Drive rating disengages the directive and restores vessel travel
durations.

### Galactic Orientation Marker Protocol

The cartographic display now presents four orientation markers at the
midpoint of each margin: Coreward at the upper edge, Rimward at the lower edge,
Spinward at the left edge, and Trailing at the right edge.
