### Starport Public Advisory

The Survey Office has authorised the issue of a Public Advisory for any charted star system. Prepared as a single printed sheet, the advisory condenses what a traveller arriving at the system might reasonably be told by Starport Control: the layout of the system, the character of its main world, the charges levied at the port, and the goods that may be bought and sold to advantage.

The advisory is intended for distribution to vessels approaching or entering the system, providing crews with a concise account of local conditions before arrival. Designed to be printed and kept close at hand, it allows travellers to consult charts, port regulations and commercial information without access to a shipboard terminal. Only information cleared for public release by the Survey Office is included, with restricted survey findings and intelligence omitted.

Public Advisories may be generated directly from the System Survey Console.
