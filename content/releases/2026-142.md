### Languages Module

The Data Cores facility now incorporates a Languages generation module, accessible via the Languages
entry in the Data Cores navigation index. The module provides procedural word generation across twelve
registered linguistic corpora: Arrghoun, Aslan, French, Gurvin, Hindi, Imperium, Japanese, Korean,
Nordic, Oynprith, Solomani, and Zdetl.
