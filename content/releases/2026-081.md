### Tidal Lock

Tidal lock relationships are now recorded and displayed across survey records.
Where a body is tidally locked to another — whether a moon to its parent
planet, a planet to its primary star, or any analogous gravitational coupling —
the locked body's orbital data section displays a reference to the target. If
the target body is subsequently removed from the survey database, the tidal
lock reference is automatically cleared.

### Configurable Sophont Hex Colours

Campaign settings now include colour assignments for systems harbouring native
sophont species and systems bearing records of extinct sophont civilisations.
These colours are applied to the hex background of affected systems across all
cartographic output — subsector maps, viewport maps, and the interactive
navigation display.

Allegiance colouring takes precedence where assigned; sophont colouring applies
only to systems not otherwise distinguished by allegiance data. Either colour
may be cleared to suppress its display entirely across all map formats. Default
colours are pre-loaded on campaign initialisation.
