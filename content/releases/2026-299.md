### Extinct Sophont Species Designation

Bodies classified as having harboured an extinct sophont may now carry a
designation of the species concerned. The species is selected from the campaign
register or entered by name, in which case a new register entry is created with
the body's system recorded as its point of origin. The designation is optional;
an extinct classification may remain unspecified. Native and extinct species
are recorded independently, and each designation field is presented only while
its corresponding classification is active.

The designation appears on survey records, in the exported Markdown record and
in the data interface. Cartographic filters and highlight rules accept a new
parameter, Extinct Sophont Species, permitting systems to be selected by the
extinct species recorded upon any of their bodies.
