### Moon Survey Records

The Astrogation Console now supports comprehensive display of satellite survey data.
Moon records present physical parameters — diameter, mass, gravity, density —
alongside environmental data including temperature, rotation, axial tilt, albedo,
and greenhouse factor. Atmospheric and hydrographic classifications, biological
survey indices, and social data are displayed where present in the survey record.

A satellite registry is appended to the survey display for each gas giant,
terrestrial planet, and other body with catalogued moons. Each entry lists the
satellite's size code, orbital distance in planetary diameters, and Universal World
Profile. If the satellite is the designated system main world, it is marked
accordingly.

### Planetoid Catalogue

A planetoid satellite catalogue has been added to the gas giant and planetoid belt
survey displays. The catalogue lists individual planetoid bodies associated with
the belt, including size classification and orbital parameters.

### Collapsible Survey Sections

Satellite registries and planetoid catalogues on body survey records may now be
collapsed to conserve display space. The section header displays a count of
catalogued entries. A filter control permits operators to restrict the satellite
registry to significant bodies only.
