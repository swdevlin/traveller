### Voyage Record Terminal

The navigation display now carries a Jump Log terminal, accessed by the arrow
symbol in the control bar. The terminal presents every recorded jump in order of
arrival, listing the vessel, the date of departure and the point of departure, then the
date of arrival and the destination. Misjumps are marked against their destination.

Referee personnel are additionally granted an Add directive, an Edit
indicator upon each entry, and an Expunge indicator upon the most recent entry
of each vessel, requiring confirmation. Upon selection of a vessel, its last recorded
position and departure date are entered automatically, and the destination is
verified against the jump rating of the vessel.
