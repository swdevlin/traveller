### Granular Tech Level Capability Registers

The Tech Level subsystem now records eleven discrete capability categories in
addition to the overall civilisation code. Operators may specify independent
tech level values for Electronics, Energy, Manufacturing, Medical,
Environmental, Land Transport, Water Transport, Air Transport, Space Transport,
Personal Military, and Heavy Military.

The Tech Level tab on stellar object records displays the full capability
matrix, each category rendered with its hex code and era descriptor drawn from
the standard tech level tables. Categories not yet assigned are suppressed from
the display. Markdown exports are likewise updated to enumerate all populated
categories with their descriptors.
