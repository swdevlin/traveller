### Satellite Display Directive for Orbital Charts

The orbital chart for a star system, and for an individual star, now accepts a
satellite display directive. Three classifications are available: None,
Significant, and All. Under None, the chart presents primary bodies only, as
before. Under Significant, satellites of consequential size are plotted; bodies
of size classification 0 or S are withheld. Under All, every recorded satellite
is plotted.
