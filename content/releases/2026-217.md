### Lexicon Retrieval Terminal

The console now interrogates the ship's library banks directly. An operator keys in a term — a
rule, a piece of equipment, a scrap of jargon — and the terminal returns a ranked manifest of
matching volumes, complete with printed page reference and an illuminated excerpt of the
surrounding text. The manifest may be narrowed by volume, edition, category, or a minimum
relevance threshold. A referee's console draws upon every volume the campaign has stocked in
its library; a player console is restricted to the subset the referee has cleared for general
access. The terminal is also reachable directly from the starmap console's toolbar.
