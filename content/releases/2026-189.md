### Survey Overlay Console

The cartographic display now supports a Survey Overlay function, permitting
operators to define colour-coded rules governing hex illumination
across the chart. Each rule evaluates conditions drawn from the operator's
survey holdings, combining them through configurable logical grouping, with
any condition subject to inversion at the operator's discretion.

### Overlay Precedence Sequencing

Where the chart's illumination rules yield conflicting results for a single
hex, operators may now establish precedence by resequencing the rule
listing. The foremost rule in the sequence governs; adjustment controls are
provided to advance or recede any rule's standing.
