### Jump Shadow Orbital Display

The orbital transit diagram now renders the jump shadow boundary for each stellar
body in the system. The exclusion zone is calibrated to each star's computed jump
shadow radius. Bodies within a stellar jump shadow are visually distinguished from
those in clear space, providing the operator with an immediate assessment of safe
jump emergence points.

### Planetoid Import Refinements

The procedural synthesis importer now correctly assigns orbital sequence identifiers
to planetoid bodies and moons generated as satellite entries.
