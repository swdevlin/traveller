### Social Characteristics Assignment

Star systems that have not yet received a sociological survey may now be
processed through the Social Characteristics Assignment terminal. Activation
of the **Social Characteristics** control on the star system record transmits
the system's stellar and orbital configuration to the generator apparatus,
which returns a fully computed main world designation together with population
magnitude, government type, law level, tech level, and starport classification.
Trade codes are assigned concurrently and propagated to both the main world
record and the star system register.

Seed parameters — main world criteria, population range, government, law level,
and tech level bounds — may be supplied to constrain the generator's output;
all fields are optional and default to standard table procedures when omitted.

The assignment control is suppressed automatically for any system in which an
inhabited world has already been recorded, preventing inadvertent overwrite of
established sociological data.

### Stellar Chart: Hex Coordinate Legibility

The four-digit parsec coordinate rendered at the apex of each hex cell on the
stellar cartography diagram has been increased in size. Field operatives
reporting difficulty reading small hex numbers under low-light conditions
should find the updated rendering materially improved.
