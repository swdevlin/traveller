### Surface Map Upload Protocol

Terrestrial planets, planetoids, and moons of size 1 or greater may now be assigned a surface map supplied by the operator. An image in PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF format, not exceeding 10 MB, is submitted from the map panel of the object's survey record and is retained exactly as supplied. An uploaded map replaces any generated map; a generated map, once requested, likewise replaces an upload. The download directive returns the image in its original format.

### Map Removal Directive

A stored surface map, whether generated or uploaded, may now be removed from an object's record by the Remove Map directive. A confirmation prompt precedes removal, as the action cannot be reversed. The staleness advisory, which warns that an object's characteristics have changed since its map was made, applies to generated maps only.
