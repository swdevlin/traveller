### Lunar Jump Shadow Telemetry

Moon detail pages now display a Jump Shadow section within Orbital Data. The
system calculates the effective shadow distance by considering the moon's own
mass, the residual shadow of the parent planet at the moon's orbital radius,
and the residual shadows of all stars in the hierarchy at cumulative distance.

### Jump Shadow Origin

The Jump Shadow section, across all world types, now identifies the body whose
shadow governs the effective clear distance. No source is shown when the 
object's own mass presents the dominant hazard to jump navigation.
