### Survey Dossier Protocol

The Survey Office is pleased to announce the issue of a printed dossier for any charted star system.

From the star system's **Actions** menu, a single-page **Handout** may now be requested. It presents the system schematic, the main world's surface chart and planetary profile, starport charges, jump shadow times, a port briefing, and the commodity purchase and sale modifiers conferred by the world's trade classifications. Matters reserved to the referee are never printed.

Notes retain their formatting, and any that run long are set smaller to fit. The dossier is prepared on Letter or A4 stock, as designated by the new **Handout Paper Size** directive in Campaign Settings, on a white page with an accent colour taken from the viewer's chosen theme.
