### Jump Log Registry — Vessel Audit Filter

The Jump Log Registry index now incorporates a vessel selection control. Operators may designate a specific ship from the filtration dropdown to restrict the displayed records to that vessel's complete transit history, facilitating rapid audit of jump sequences, departure and arrival datestamps, and misjump incidents for any single craft. Selecting the null directive — *All ships* — restores the full multi-vessel registry view.
