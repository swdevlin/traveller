### Jump Log Registry — Vessel Audit Filter

The Jump Log Registry index now incorporates a vessel selection control. Operators may designate a specific ship from the filtration dropdown to restrict the displayed records to that vessel's complete transit history, facilitating rapid audit of jump sequences, departure and arrival datestamps, and misjump incidents for any single craft. Selecting the null directive — *All ships* — restores the full multi-vessel registry view.

### Linguistic Name Generation Protocol

All designation input fields — sectors, subsectors, star systems, and stellar objects — now incorporate a Linguistic Name Generation unit. Two auxiliary controls appear adjacent to the name field: a dice actuator and a language selector.

Activating the dice actuator submits a request to the Word Generation Engine, which returns a procedurally constructed designation formed according to the assigned linguistic parameters. The language selector presents the full catalogue of available tongues; the operator may assign a specific language to the object or select *Inherited* to delegate upward through the object hierarchy — stellar objects inherit from their parent star system, star systems from their subsector, subsectors from their sector, and sectors from the campaign default.

A campaign-wide default language may be configured via the new **Name Generation** section of Campaign Settings. When no explicit language is set at any level in the hierarchy, the dice actuator remains inoperative.
