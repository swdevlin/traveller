### Travel Zone Classification

Star systems may now be assigned a travel zone, providing standardised hazard
classification visible across the interface. Two canonical zones are pre-installed
in all campaigns: Amber (exercise caution) and Red (interdicted or extremely
dangerous). These protected classifications cannot be deleted. Additional zones
may be defined under Data Cores → Travel Zones, each assigned a display colour.
