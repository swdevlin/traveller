### Public Map Access via Signed URLs

Sector, subsector, and star system maps may now be accessed without an active
session via a signed URL. A copy-to-clipboard icon is displayed adjacent to
each map; activating it places the signed URL on the clipboard for immediate
deployment.

The signed URL may be embedded directly into world-construction platforms such
as World Anvil, Legend Keeper, and Obsidian, or inserted into the image
display systems of virtual tabletop installations including Foundry VTT and
Roll20. The cartographic data unit will update automatically as the underlying
survey records are modified, requiring no manual re-export or file transfer
operation. Maps served to unauthenticated terminals are rendered without
interactive navigation hyperlinks.
