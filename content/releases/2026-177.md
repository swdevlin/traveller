### IISS Survey Record Re-Processing

Survey operators may now submit a terrestrial planet or planetoid belt record
for re-processing against its Universal World Profile. Upon invocation, the
Imperial Grand Survey data core recomputes the full characteristic profile
from the recorded UWP and orbital parameters, superseding all previously
filed survey data. This procedure is indicated where field corrections have
been applied to the UWP or where initial survey data is considered
provisional.
