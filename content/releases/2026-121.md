### Visual Theme Matrix

The display subsystem now supports a configurable visual theme register. Operators
may select from a range of chromatic profiles drawn from the major sophont cultures
of Charted Space: Aslan, Bwap, Darrian, Droyne, Hiver, Imperium, K'kree, Solomani, 
Sword Worlds, Vargr, Vegan, Zhodani, and the Deepnight void-transit
scheme. The active theme is recorded in the operator's local session register and
persists across subsequent connections.

### Cartographic Centring Protocol

The integrated star map now centres its display viewport on the stellar object or
star system record currently under inspection. When navigating directly to a
system, subsector, or stellar object, the cartographic display locks its origin
coordinates to the associated parsec position, eliminating manual repositioning
of the chart following record retrieval.
