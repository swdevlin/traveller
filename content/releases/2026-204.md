### Transit Time Registers

The route plotting terminal now computes transit duration for every waypoint
along a charted course — the interval required to travel between a world's
surface and the safe jump distance beyond it. Console operators select the
vessel's manoeuvre drive rating, and each estimate adjusts accordingly. Every
waypoint carries a running elapsed-time figure, and the completed course
record itemises total jump duration, total transit duration, and their
combination, each rendered together with a probable margin of error.
