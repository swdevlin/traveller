### Stellar Chart: Transit Time to Jump Threshold

Each body displayed on the stellar cartography diagram now carries an elapsed
time readout indicating the duration required to reach safe jump threshold from
the object's surface. Figures are rendered to the left of the body node and
are computed assuming M-Drive 4 constant-acceleration thrust throughout transit.

### Directional Input: Chart Navigation Protocol

The stellar cartography console now accepts directional input via the navigator's
keyboard array. Depression of a cardinal arrow key advances the chart display by
one parsec in the indicated heading. Simultaneous activation of the CTRL modifier
engages rapid traverse mode, displacing the viewport by one half the visible
sector extent — distance scaled to the current magnification factor. Input is
suppressed whilst an analysis overlay is engaged or the full-journey projection
is active.
